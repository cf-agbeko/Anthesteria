About the Event

This one-day Greek cultural event in Naiposha Gardens, Kenya, offers a vibrant celebration of ancient Greek culture, art, tradition and food bringing the spirit of the renowned Anthesteria Festival to East Africa. The program will include a dynamic mix of theatrical productions, live music with food and wine pairing and captivating dance performances inspired by Greek mythology. The festival will also include interactive activities like flower arrangements, pottery and a pop-up market. Visitors will enjoy authentic Greek cuisine served fresh with a relative wine to pair. The event will foster a sense of community and cultural exchange, echoing the ancient tradition of the Epidaurus Festival, where performances were believed to have therapeutic and unifying powers. With a capacity for 70 guests, the intimate setting ensures a personalized and memorable experience, combining the grandeur of ancient drama with the natural beauty of Naiposha Gardens.
